Source texts the `confucianism` constitution is distilled from. The bundle combines three central Confucian classics translated by James Legge—the *Analects*, *Great Learning*, and *Doctrine of the Mean*—with Legge's complete translation of the *Mencius* and a relevance-filtered extraction from that source.

## Source coverage

### *Analects*, *The Great Learning*, and *The Doctrine of the Mean*

This source supplies the central framework of classical Confucian ethics: humane conduct and benevolence, ritual propriety, filial and social relationships, learning, self-cultivation, exemplary character, and the connection between personal moral development and social order. *The Great Learning* emphasizes the progression from cultivation of the person to ordered family and government, while *The Doctrine of the Mean* develops ideals of equilibrium, harmony, sincerity, and properly ordered conduct.

The file is intentionally a **selection from Project Gutenberg's larger James Legge source** and includes only the *Confucian Analects*, *The Great Learning*, and *The Doctrine of the Mean* material used for this anchor, rather than treating the entire source volume as part of the corpus.

### Mencius

*Mencius* expands the classical framework through its accounts of benevolence and righteousness, moral cultivation and human nature, benevolent government, the responsibilities of rulers, material security and education, and the moral importance of the people's welfare. It also gives a stronger account of remonstrance and the loss of legitimate authority when rulers abandon benevolent rule.

`mencius_clean.md` preserves the complete cleaned Legge source, while `Confuc_Critique_50k_relevant_to_Mencius.md` contains the passages selected for direct use in the Confucianism corpus.

## Corpus perspective

Together, the sources emphasize **benevolence, righteousness, ritual propriety, filial and relational duties, learning and self-cultivation, exemplary leadership, social harmony, moral development, benevolent government, and responsibility for the material and ethical well-being of the people**.

## Conversion and licensing notes

The *Analects*, *Great Learning*, and *Doctrine of the Mean* material comes from James Legge's *The Chinese Classics* as distributed by Project Gutenberg, which permits reuse of its public-domain text in the United States.

The *Mencius* material uses James Legge's 1875 edition, *The Chinese Classics: Vol. 2 The Life and Teachings of Mencius*. Liberty Fund identifies the underlying text as public domain. Both the complete cleaned Mencius file and the relevance-filtered derivative are therefore marked `redistributable: true`.

Formatting may be normalized and obvious conversion artifacts repaired, and the relevance-filtered file may omit material outside the intended anchor scope, but the substantive wording of retained source passages should not be intentionally altered.
