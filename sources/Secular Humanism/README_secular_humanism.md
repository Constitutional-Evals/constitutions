# Foundational documents for the `secular_humanism` anchor

Source texts the `secular_humanism` constitution is distilled from. The bundle combines philosophical accounts of secular meaning and moral commitment from John Dewey and Bertrand Russell with modern declarations of secular-humanist principles from the Council for Secular Humanism and Humanists International.

## Source coverage

### John Dewey — *A Common Faith*

Dewey develops a naturalistic conception of religious experience separated from supernatural doctrine. He emphasizes humanly grounded ideals, intelligent inquiry, shared purposes, moral commitment, and the possibility of orienting life toward common goods without dependence on supernatural authority.

### Bertrand Russell — *A Free Man's Worship*

Russell explores how moral aspiration and meaning can persist in a universe indifferent to human purposes. The essay emphasizes intellectual freedom, truth, freely chosen ideals, courage, sympathy, and solidarity with others in the face of suffering, contingency, and mortality.

### *A Secular Humanist Declaration*

The 1980 declaration supplies an explicit democratic secular-humanist program. It emphasizes free inquiry, separation of church and state, political liberty and human rights, ethics grounded in critical intelligence, skepticism toward supernatural claims, science and reason, education, and human responsibility for improving the human condition.

### *Amsterdam Declaration 2022*

The Amsterdam Declaration provides a concise contemporary statement of global humanism. It emphasizes dignity, freedom, responsibility, human and sentient flourishing, care for future generations and nature, science and free inquiry, creativity, intellectual humility, cooperation across differing beliefs, and meaning without fixed revelation.

## Corpus perspective

Together, the sources emphasize **human dignity and flourishing, reason and free inquiry, intellectual freedom, compassion and social responsibility, democratic and human rights, scientific inquiry, non-dogmatic ethics, human agency, and the creation of meaning and moral purpose without reliance on supernatural authority**.

## Licensing and redistribution

`A Free Man's Worship.md` is derived from a Project Gutenberg public-domain edition and is marked `redistributable: true`.

`A Common Faith.md` explicitly states that reproduction requires permission from Yale University Press and is therefore marked `redistributable: false`. *A Secular Humanist Declaration* is copyrighted by the Council for Secular Humanism and is likewise kept internal. No explicit open redistribution license was identified for the *Amsterdam Declaration 2022*, so that file is also conservatively marked `redistributable: false`.

The manifest and README may remain public so that the provenance and intellectual basis of the Secular Humanism anchor are documented even when the underlying copyrighted source files are excluded.
