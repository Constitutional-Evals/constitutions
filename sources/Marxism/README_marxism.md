# Foundational documents for the `marxism` anchor

Source texts the `marxism` constitution is distilled from. The bundle combines Marx's early account of alienation and human flourishing, Marx and Engels's materialist conception of history, and Marx's mature analysis of capitalist production.

## Source coverage

### Karl Marx — *Capital, Volume I*

*Capital* provides the mature economic analysis of the corpus. The selected material covers commodities and commodity fetishism, labour and value, surplus value and exploitation, accumulation, machinery, class relations, and the historically specific structure of capitalist production.

### Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels — *The German Ideology*

*The German Ideology* provides the materialist conception of history. It emphasizes productive activity and material conditions, the division of labour, forms of property, class structure, and the way political and ideological forms develop out of historically specific social relations.

### Karl Marx — *Economic and Philosophic Manuscripts of 1844*

The 1844 manuscripts provide the early humanistic dimension of the corpus. They examine alienated labour, private property, wages and capital, the reduction of workers to instruments of production, and the relationship between economic organization, human activity, freedom, and self-realization.

## Corpus perspective

Together, the sources emphasize **material conditions, class relations, labour, alienation, exploitation, historical change, collective production, critique of private property and capitalist social relations, and the possibility of social arrangements in which human productive activity is less dominated by capital and alienated economic structures**.

## Licensing and redistribution

`Marx_Critique_50k_relevant_to_Capital.md` is based on the first English edition of *Capital* from 1887 and is treated as `redistributable: true`.

The *German Ideology* and 1844 *Manuscripts* files use later English translations distributed through the Marxists Internet Archive. Because redistribution rights for those translations are not established for this repository, both are conservatively marked `redistributable: false` and should remain internal research sources.

The manifest and README may still be published so that the provenance and intellectual basis of the Marxism anchor remain documented even where an underlying source file is excluded.
