# Foundational documents for `care-ethics`

This bundle follows the technical appendix's Care ethics row: Carol Gilligan,
*In a Different Voice* (1982); Nel Noddings, *Caring* (1984); and Virginia
Held, *The Ethics of Care* (2006).

The appendix's three original books are copyrighted and remain metadata-only.
The bundle additionally includes Hamington and FitzGerald's 2022 CC BY 4.0
article, which gives a usable, contemporary account of care ethics, its
relational orientation, and its relation to Gilligan's work.
