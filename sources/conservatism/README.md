# Foundational documents for `conservatism`

This bundle follows the technical appendix's Conservatism row: Edmund Burke,
*Reflections on the Revolution in France* (1790); Michael Oakeshott,
*Rationalism in Politics* (1962); and Roger Scruton, *How to Be a
Conservative* (2014).

## Committed sources

`burke-reflections-selected.md` is a public-domain, thematically selected
edition of Burke's *Reflections*. It focuses on the passages that ground the
constitution's emphasis on circumstance, inherited institutions, prudence,
piecemeal reform, and obligations across generations. The selection is well
below the 50k-token per-document limit.

`tocqueville-democracy-associations.md` is Tocqueville's public-domain chapter
on political associations. It supplies a second primary source for the role of
local and voluntary association in social continuity.

## Copyright

Burke's 1790 work and Tocqueville's 1835 work are public domain and may be
distributed here. Oakeshott and Scruton are modern copyrighted works, so their
entries remain metadata-only in `manifest.json`; the public-domain texts are
the usable source bundle.
